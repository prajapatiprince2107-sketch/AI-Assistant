
SHALU AI 12.5 — EMOTIONAL CONVERSATION UPGRADE
================================================

The goal of this version is to make Shalu feel less robotic and more like a
friendly anime assistant: dynamic pauses, sentence-level emotion, subtle
pitch/rate changes, automatic expressions, blinking, and mouth movement.

The uploaded reference video was used as a STYLE target only. The app does not
clone or copy the speaker's voice.

IMPORTANT
---------
This still uses the browser's SpeechSynthesis engine. The biggest quality
difference comes from choosing an online/natural voice such as a Microsoft
"Online (Natural)" female voice when your PC/browser provides one.

Start:
    START_SHALU_12_5.bat

Then select:
    Voice -> Auto Natural (recommended)
    Conversation -> Emotional / Natural

No ElevenLabs API key is required.
